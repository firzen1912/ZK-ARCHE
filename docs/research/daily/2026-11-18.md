## 2. Executive synthesis

1. **Authenticate transition state, not merely handshake values.** The SSH strict-KEX mitigation illustrates why unauthenticated packet sequencing can invalidate later integrity even with a nominal key exchange. This is a cross-protocol *test hypothesis*, not evidence of a ZK-ARCHE vulnerability; SSH-style sequence resets must not be copied into ZK-ARCHE.
2. **Actor possession is not message-effect approval.** The WIMSE proposal distinguishes a canonical message hash, receiver audience and verifier nonce. ZK-ARCHE ENROLL needs an independently testable content/operation/authority-to-durable-effect identity invariant, including relayed or reserialized requests.
3. **Single-use selective disclosure is not reusable anonymity.** A traditional-signature JWP can disclose selected signed payloads but is linkable if repeatedly presented; avoiding reuse requires fresh issuance, individual holder keys and offline inventory. Treat it as an optional measured comparator against BBS, never as an automatic replacement.

### D20261118-F01 — A strong key exchange cannot compensate for unauthenticated state splicing across the KEX frontier

- **Novelty / owner:** refines; R-009 P0, R-011 cross-reference.
- **Primary source / publication / type:** IETF SSHM WG, *SSH Strict KEX extension*, `draft-ietf-sshm-strict-kex-03`, **2026-11-17**, active Standards Track Internet-Draft (not an RFC), https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-sshm-strict-kex-03 .
- **Verified source-supported fact:** The SSH Terrapin scenario can exploit pre-KEX injected, otherwise ignorable messages that affect implicit packet sequence numbers without authenticating those changes into the exchange hash, resulting in initial protected-packet truncation for affected cipher configurations. The SSH draft mitigates this by limiting initial-KEX messages and resetting sequence counters at KEX completion. The underlying attack predates this draft revision.
- **Exact owner / anchor:** R-009, TD-003/TD-004; `spec/zk-arche-protocol.md`, `spec/negotiation-selection-state-machine.md`, `spec/replay-continuity.md`; `rust/crates/proto/src/auth_v3.rs` and `c/src/proto/auth_v3.c` and replay-continuity counterparts.
- **Bottleneck:** A packet accepted/rejected at the unauthenticated-to-protected boundary can affect replay state, association continuation and application effects without belonging to the cryptographic AUTH transcript. SSH's implicit transport sequence design is not demonstrated in ZK-ARCHE.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Local paired Rust/C transition-frontier oracle: insert ignorable or invalid pre-AUTH messages, duplicates, delayed retries, and cross-session packets; force first protected-effect loss, reordering and replay; compare legitimate positive trace with one-change mutants. Verify no untrusted packet controls authenticated session/authorization state, no hidden sequence counter continuity assumptions, consistent classification of parser rejects versus failed AEAD verification, and no inconsistent commit after first-message loss. Never import an SSH-specific sequence reset.
- **Evidence maturity:** externally-reviewed source-protocol security experience; **concept** for ZK-ARCHE applicability. No local reproduction or proof.
- **Limitations:** SSH is a different framing, KEX, cipher and transport protocol. This is not a ZK-ARCHE exploit finding.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** A cross-layer transcript-to-protected-effect audit is appropriate before making end-to-end agreement or replay claims.
- **Wire / CPU / RAM / flash / dependency / trust / privacy:** Zero baseline wire/dependency impact. Local test harness overhead only; bounded pre-session resource/availability effects may later need profiling, without extra mandatory trust.
- **Rust/C / vector / profile compatibility:** Preserve v2, suite `0x0001` and non-selectable v3. Produce paired positive/negative exact-head trace fixtures and model-to-code mapping, not a changed handshake wire.
- **Required next evidence / disposition:** Exact-head two-lane executable boundary and first-protected-effect matrix, explicit v2/v3 separation, independent discrepancy review. **reproduce**.
- **Weekly finding candidate:** yes.

### D20261118-F02 — A valid actor proof still needs an immutable operation-content and effect identity

- **Novelty / owner:** refines R-008 P1, R-012 cross-reference.
- **Primary source / publication / type:** Nandakumar/Jennings, *Message Binding for Workload Proof Tokens*, `draft-nandakumar-wimse-wpt-message-binding-00`, **2026-11-16**, **individual** Internet-Draft (not WG-adopted specification or RFC), https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-nandakumar-wimse-wpt-message-binding-00 .
- **Verified source-supported fact:** WPT `mth` contains a SHA-256 hash under a named canonicalization profile, audience `aud` identifies the receiver, and verifier `nonce` supports freshness despite TLS-terminating intermediaries. Unrecognized profiles or content hash mismatches cause rejection. The credential member is excluded from the content hash to avoid self-reference. The draft explicitly cautions that transport metadata may not be protected; repeated actions need separate idempotency semantics, and clock-based claims depend on trustworthy time.
- **Exact owner / anchor:** R-008, TD-004, `spec/enrollment-grant-issuance.md`, `spec/enroll-lifecycle-state-machine.md`, `spec/authorization-generation-lifecycle.md`; `rust/crates/proto/src/enrollment_grant.rs` and `c/src/proto/enrollment_grant.c`.
- **Bottleneck:** Current wire-neutral enrollment classifiers check commissioner identity/authority, generation, nonce, subject PoP and rollback, but a byte-exact immutable operation identity through relays and durable commit requires separate corpus evidence. Secure transport is not permission to change trust.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Create a **test-only canonical operation descriptor** with operation kind, issuer/authority namespace, audience, subject key commitment, requested rights, current predecessor/generation, operation ID, nonce and exact body bytes. Match positive reference against substituted payload, duplicated/reordered fields, unknown canonicalizer, wrong audience, recycled nonce, missing predecessor, altered relay metadata, and commit after revocation. Test authorize-at-start versus authorize-at-effect under crash, replay and partial writes. Preserve current issuer-controlled trust mutation and NO-LEARNING ordinary AUTH.
- **Evidence maturity:** concept; individual Internet-Draft; no application-specific formal proof or MCU measurement.
- **Limitations:** JWT/JCS/JSON relays do not define ZK-ARCHE binary framing. A signed hash alone does not guarantee idempotency, current rights, durable replay continuity or confidentiality.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Operation-content binding and authorization effect-frontier must be separate testable properties, even when actor possession is valid.
- **Wire / CPU / RAM / flash / dependency / trust / privacy:** Baseline zero. If a future separately transmitted SHA-256 digest were adopted, minimum 32 extra bytes before headers/nonce/identifier; SHA-256 work linear in content length; durable nonce/operation IDs cost RAM/flash/writes. Visible identifiers may correlate sessions. No cloud/PKI/JWT requirement.
- **Rust/C / vectors / profile:** v2 and draft-v3 unchanged; compare exact canonical byte inputs, authorized positive and one-field-mutation rejects in both languages; bound malformed-content parsing on MCU.
- **Required next evidence / disposition:** Local Rust/C operation-descriptor and relay mutation matrix, cross-crash/rollback consistency, MCU storage/flash measurements, no production wire adoption. **reproduce**.
- **Weekly finding candidate:** yes.

### D20261118-F03 — Single-use selective disclosure is an offline-issuer inventory choice, not reusable anonymity

- **Novelty / owner:** refines R-002 P3; R-015 P0 privacy cross-reference.
- **Primary sources / publication / type:** JOSE WG, *JSON Web Proof* `draft-ietf-jose-json-web-proof-15` and *JSON Proof Algorithms* `draft-ietf-jose-json-proof-algorithms-15`, both **2026-11-16**, active Standards Track Internet-Drafts (not RFCs), https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-jose-json-web-proof-15 and https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-jose-json-proof-algorithms-15 .
- **Verified source-supported fact:** JWP supports selective disclosure with algorithm-specific proofs. JPA's Single Use (`SU`) profile signs the issued header and separate payloads with traditional asymmetric signatures and is **linkable when the same issued JWP is presented repeatedly**. To maintain unlinkability a holder needs a new JWP from the issuer (possibly in batches) and per-JWP holder presentation keys; JPA also describes pairing-based BBS as a separate algorithm. JWP container `aud` and `nonce` are optional and therefore application policy must require and validate them where needed.
- **Exact owner / anchor:** R-002 and R-015; `spec/privacy-considerations.md`, `spec/iot-profiles.md`, `spec/registries.md` and current native `0x0001` suite/role-proof source; none of the optional JWP modes is implemented as mandatory ZK-ARCHE.
- **Bottleneck:** Choosing traditional signatures to avoid pairing complexity may require issuer connectivity, preloaded single-use proofs/keys and additional persistent storage. Selective disclosure alone does not imply session unlinkability.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Three controlled lanes: (A) existing NO-LEARNING role proof; (B) per-use SU-JWP signatures with 1, 10 and 100 offline presentations, preloaded inventory and fresh holder presentation keys; (C) randomized BBS presentations with the same disclosed attributes and authorization scope. Measure complete issued/presented wire, issuer and device CPU, RAM/stack, flash inventory, entropy/key lifecycle, presentation/issuance latency, energy, expired/preloaded credentials, revoked credential lookup, observer correlation and colluding-verifier inference. Include repeated-SU negative privacy class and holder PoP/nonce/audience mismatch rejects.
- **Evidence maturity:** concept for ZK-ARCHE; current WG draft source descriptions, not independent library/security review or MCU benchmark.
- **Limitations:** JOSE drafts can change, conventional signing cost depends on platform, and BBS pairing/suite cost is unknown here. Neither source establishes ZK-ARCHE privacy or production readiness.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Compare optional schemes as concrete privacy+resource+issuer-availability profiles, not one universally superior primitive.
- **Wire / CPU / RAM / flash / dependency / trust / privacy:** No baseline impact; SU adds individual signature bytes, holder key storage and replenishment state; BBS adds pairing curve/library dependencies and prover/verifier memory/CPU. All exact MCU costs unmeasured. SU reuse exposes stable proof-level correlation.
- **Rust/C / vector / profile:** No native-v2/v3 change. Any optional new suite/profile needs byte-exact presentation/nonce/PoP, algorithm-confusion, re-use/cross-verifier and revocation-negative vectors, complete implementation and physical MCU/edge benchmarks, plus explicit human acceptance.
- **Required next evidence / disposition:** Version-pinned SU/BBS full-message and 1/10/100-use comparative hardware/issuer-online/offline corpus with Rust/C parity; **benchmark** as next task, R-002 remains `researching` until results exist.
