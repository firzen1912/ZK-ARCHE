## 2. Executive synthesis

1. The August 2026 CFRG Sigma Protocols -03 draft describes linear-map preimage proofs and **explicitly excludes OR and threshold composition** from its specified algorithms. ZK-ARCHE's CDS role-set proof is an OR composition: citing this draft cannot imply its custom composition has been specified, verified, or reviewed.
2. The companion Fiat–Shamir -03 draft makes application/session domain, exact statement encoding, prefix-free codecs, canonical decoding, and no trailing unparsed proof bytes separate obligations. ZK-ARCHE's current role challenge binds pid, client nonce/ephemeral key, rerandomized commitment, ordered role entries, and per-branch commitments. It does not independently show all enclosing protocol/suite/profile/authority semantics are bound; equivalence must be traced, not presumed.
3. Existing Rust and C provers have real-versus-simulated branch schedules determined by the hidden role index. The CFRG drafts' constant-time/private-instance caution reinforces a **separate leakage-evidence requirement**, not an assertion of an exploitable side channel. Fixed total random bytes for a fixed role count do not imply fixed per-index control flow.
4. No local Rust/C build, proof, timing benchmark or physical device measurement was executed by this read-only-context research pass. R-007 stays P0/reproduce; this report sharpens the contract without promoting AUTH-v3, a cryptographic claim, or any source change.

## 5. Findings

### D20261026-F01 — Sigma linear-relation guidance is not a CDS OR-composition security proof

- **Novelty and owner:** refines R-007 (P0), TD-001; zk207/zk209/zk225/zk241.
- **Primary source, date, type:** Orrù/Yun, *Sigma Proofs for Linear Relations*, IRTF CFRG draft-irtf-cfrg-sigma-protocols-03, **17 August 2026**, active Internet-Draft (not an RFC nor IETF-endorsed standard): https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-irtf-cfrg-sigma-protocols-03 , sections 1, 4, 7 and 7.5.
- **Verified source claim:** The draft specifies proofs of preimages of linear maps in prime-order groups; it notes that **OR/threshold composition is possible but outside its specification**, with separate soundness and zero-knowledge caveats. Its security discussion and vector corpus belong to the described construction, not automatically to arbitrary CDS disjunctions.
- **Repository fact / bottleneck:** Rust and C independently compute Y_i = C' - G*role_i, simulate all but one branch, and close a shared hash-derived challenge using a true witness response; they then check challenge sums and branch equations. Current general-purpose symbolic AUTH models abstract this group-level proof. The repo has not retained an independent security proof of its full CDS OR transformation; TD-001 remains open.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Produce a **proof-obligation ledger** that separates (a) per-branch linear relation completeness/special soundness/simulator distribution, (b) OR composition and challenge-sum security, (c) Fiat–Shamir random-oracle and exact-statement context binding, (d) role-index anonymity and execution noninterference, (e) exact Rust/C and backend/target correspondence. For each property record formal argument, threat model, assumptions, concrete negative vectors and external-review owner. Do not inherit a theorem because the same group is used.
- **Evidence maturity:** external standards-track *work-in-progress specification* (conceptual/technical); **concept** for ZK-ARCHE CDS composition. No local computational security proof reproduced today.
- **Limitations:** The draft is not an OR-proof standard; it does not establish failure of ZK-ARCHE either. A theorem for one Sigma relation does not guarantee soundness or witness indistinguishability of the disjunction after noninteractive compilation.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Preserve current suite/role semantics while deriving a protocol-specific OR simulator/soundness analysis and constructing shared Rust/C cases for role-set duplicates, ordering changes, absent/invalid role, invalid commitments, modified branch challenge and modified c/s scalar, and cross-context replay.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy impact:** **none immediately** (research-only). Any future replacement of proof composition could increase proof bytes, scalar multiplications and RAM; quantify on STM32/ESP32-S3 before adopting. The main trust/privacy impact is keeping an unreviewed role-hiding claim explicitly unqualified.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile compatibility:** Current vectors and profile remain unchanged. Future local negative corpus should pin exact draft revision, dependency/backend versions and both implementers' accepted bytes, failure classes, and canonical role sequence.
- **Required next evidence / disposition / weekly candidate:** property-by-property independent CDS proof review and exact-head differential negative vectors; **reproduce / yes**.

### D20261026-F02 — Fiat–Shamir framing and private execution are distinct from mathematical transcript equality

- **Novelty and owner:** refines R-007, cross-links R-004 and TD-001/TD-003.
- **Primary source, date, type:** Orrù, *Fiat-Shamir Transformation*, IRTF CFRG draft-irtf-cfrg-fiat-shamir-03, **17 August 2026**, active non-final Internet-Draft: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-irtf-cfrg-fiat-shamir-03 , especially §§5.1–5.2, 6.1–6.2, 8.2–8.5. Companion sigma -03 above supplies the protocol-composition limitation.
- **Verified source claim:** The draft separates a protocol/suite/codec/application-distinguishing session ID from the encoded statement/instance, requires unambiguous/prefix-free input encodings and canonical decoding, requires proof parsing to consume all expected bytes, and recommends constant-time execution where private instance data may be involved. Its concrete sponge/XOF scheme and test vectors are not ZK-ARCHE's adopted Fiat–Shamir algorithm.
- **Repository fact / bottleneck:** Exact Rust role challenge absorbs pid, nonce_c, eph_c, c_prime, each ordered role r_i, and each branch A_i under T_ROLE_SET. C's role_set_master_challenge follows the same structure. The full enclosing profile/suite/method/issuer semantics are not explicit arguments to that function, so their binding requires a documented source-to-source/higher-layer implication proof, rather than treating their absence as a proven exploit. Both provers choose true_index from the private role and do one random draw/multiplication for the real branch versus two draws/two scalar multiplications plus a point subtraction for simulated branches; true branch location therefore changes per-index RNG and operation scheduling.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Create a **two-axis execution/encoding corpus**: (1) canonical serialized instance and proof-field coverage for each accepted role count, role order, duplicate and mutation case, plus application/suite/profile/peer-binding equivalence or fail-closed non-equivalence; (2) instrument Rust/C per-branch RNG-call counts, scalar operations, memory access and timing across hidden-role classes at fixed public inputs and role-list sizes. Include separate total-bytes and per-index schedules, build artifacts, target compiler/optimization and physical timing/power.
- **Evidence maturity:** draft technical guidance **externally reviewed only as IRTF work in progress**; ZK-ARCHE result **concept** based on source reading, not measured leakage.
- **Limitations:** Source-level branch differences do not prove a practical remote timing oracle; physical microarchitectural attacks, compiler transformations, interrupts, backend optimizations and observer access must be separately established. Draft session-ID derivation cannot be copied blindly into a deployed existing protocol without compatibility analysis. A constant-time group primitive does not make a secret-dependent *caller* constant-time.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Require exact input-label and length-delimitation mapping, reject trailing bytes and noncanonical scalars/points, prove role-set representation unambiguous, and distinguish nonce freshness from proof-system domain separation. Exercise cross-application and cross-profile replay under the actual accepted v2 / nonselectable v3 boundary. Test fixed-role-set hidden-witness timing with controlled RNG traces first, physical boards later.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy impact:** no immediate protocol wire or dependency change; audit adds off-target CPU/logging. Mitigation may increase worst-case per-branch group work, RNG cost, MCU RAM/flash and energy; quantify before decision. Privacy benefit remains unverified until role-index leakage measurements. Trust/authorization semantics must not be strengthened based solely on matching hashes.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile compatibility:** pin transcript bytes, order/canonical scalar and point encodings, paired Rust/C verifier decisions, role-index traces and target environment. No source/test/vector/profile change authorized; R-011 critical extension hash conflict remains a **separate** AUTH-v3 conformance blocker.
- **Required next evidence / disposition / weekly candidate:** exact-head staged transcript comparison and instrumented Rust/C witness-index traces with physical measurements, external review of any missing binding implications; **reproduce / yes**.
