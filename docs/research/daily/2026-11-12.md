## 2. Executive synthesis

1. LAKE WG RA-EDHOC -07 separates an Attester, Verifier and Relying Party. Its message_3 signed evidence uses an early transcript-derived binder; its message_4 evidence uses a completed EDHOC exporter. Neither represents ZK-ARCHE's current TLS exporter label nor automatically grants AUTH, ENROLL or DATA authority.
2. Optional attestation introduces an additional peer-key/attestation-key/verifier-key attribution problem. A valid signature and apparently matching transcript must not override a separately authorized audience, subject, commissioner, revocation state or generation.
3. LAKE EAD_1 and early EAD_2 negotiation expose evidence-format/verifier/device class under specified attacker models even if later evidence is protected. This is a potential *optional* privacy regression that must be measured against an attestation-free baseline, not an existing ZK-ARCHE vulnerability.
4. A verifier-signed cached Passport result may depend on timestamp/expiry, but a clockless constrained device cannot treat untrusted wall time as reliable freshness or authorization. Restart and rollback need independently qualified evidence.
5. The November 2 draft is new to today's reviewed R-006/R-015 research context. This run did not independently establish which clauses were revised since -06. No new standard, code, proof, formal or physical measurement claim was made.

## 5. Findings

### D20261112-F01 — Session-bound attestation is not sufficient authorization

- **Novelty and backlog owner:** new mapping to R-006; R-008/R-013 cross-reference; no source-claimed ZK-ARCHE exploit.
- **Source/date/type:** LAKE WG, *Remote attestation over EDHOC*, `draft-ietf-lake-ra-07`, **November 2, 2026**, Standards Track Internet-Draft (work in progress), https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-lake-ra-07 . Foundational primary sources: RFC 9334 (RATS, January 2023), https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334.html ; RFC 9711 (EAT, April 2025), https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9711.html .
- **Verified source claim:** The LAKE draft separates Attester (generates evidence), Verifier (appraises using attestation key/reference values) and Relying Party (decides action). The background verifier supplies an unpredictable attestation nonce of at least eight bytes. Signed COSE_Sign1 evidence carried in `message_3` uses the early binder `HKDF-Expand(0, [H(H(message_1), message_2), "attestation", ID_CRED_I], hash_length)` as external AAD: the binder input is an early transcript with an all-zero PRK, **not a secret completed exporter**. Evidence in `message_4` uses `EDHOC_Exporter(label=2, context="attestation", length=hash_length)` instead. EAD/attestation semantics are specific to this draft.
- **Owner/anchor:** R-006, R-008, R-013 / TD-004; `spec/auth-trust-mutation-boundary.md`, `spec/security-considerations.md`, `spec/transport-binding-and-adapter-authority.md`, Rust grant and C binding classifiers.
- **Bottleneck:** A valid device-measurement signature does not independently establish that the signer represents the locally authenticated and authorized peer, correct audience, current revocation generation or trustworthy verifier. The early binder cannot be substituted for final AUTH Finished or a TLS exporter.
- **Distinct idea:** Optional **two-key/three-role attribution matrix** for `(authenticated_peer_key, attestation_signing_key, verifier_key, current_exact_session, nonce, evidence_kind, policy/audience, measurement_reference_version, current_authorization_generation)`. Positive: matching keys, authorized verifier and same session. Negative: foreign correctly signed attestation, replay of genuine evidence into second session, wrong nonce/transcript/peer, unauthorized verifier, stale measurement, different audience, revoked credential, invalid commissioner, attempted trust-store mutation and DATA effect. All negatives must deny without changing trusted state.
- **Evidence maturity:** `concept` draft; pre-existing ZK-ARCHE classifier `software` source does not test this combined matrix.
- **Limitations:** Early-public-transcript binder alone is not proof of a security flaw: the evidence is signed by the attestation key. Correct composition depends on the signature, exact actor identity, authenticated AKE and verifier. The particular -06→-07 changes were not established; no runtime implementation exists in scope.
- **Inference:** If ever adopted, attestation is a versioned **optional** signal feeding independent local authorization. It must not introduce a mandatory CA, online verifier, cloud service, hardware root or auto-learning during core P2P AUTH.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy:** Existing baseline none. Optional nonce/evidence/signature/binder/verifier identity add bytes/fragmentation, signature/hash/CBOR processing CPU/flash, claim buffering RAM, reference/nonce state storage, attestation-key and verifier trust. Stable device measurements/keys may increase linkability. All costs unmeasured.
- **Rust/C/vectors/profile impact:** Existing 0x0001 profile and vectors untouched. Optional prototype would require pinned proof/cipher/encoding/credential mapping and Rust/C positive and cross-key/session/replay/issuer/nonce negative vectors, with MCU sizing and independent review.
- **Next evidence / disposition:** Human-approved optional threat model; exact-head paired Rust/C attestation-key and verifier-policy oracles plus physical MCU cost. `research-only`; weekly candidate yes.

### D20261112-F02 — EAD negotiation/privacy and signed-cache currentness are separate assurance questions

- **Novelty and backlog owner:** `refines` R-015, R-006 cross-reference, R-005 measured-resource cross-reference.
- **Source/date/type:** LAKE WG, `draft-ietf-lake-ra-07` **November 2, 2026** §§5, 9, Appendix B; RATS RFC 9334 January 2023; EAT RFC 9711 April 2025. Primary links: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-lake-ra-07 , https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9334.html .
- **Verified source claim:** EDHOC EAD_1 lacks passive and active privacy; EAD_2 encrypted negotiation can be vulnerable to an active attacker before initiator authentication. The draft says sensitive evidence must wait for EAD_3 under RFC 9528's four methods, or EAD_4 under its PSK draft profile. Its Passport model permits cached verifier-signed results subject to timestamps/expiry and leaves time synchronization out of scope. It discusses re-attestation on session resumption or later protected application exchange. These are LAKE comparators, **not current ZK-ARCHE packet observations**.
- **Owner/anchor:** R-015, R-006, zk218/zk222, `spec/privacy-considerations.md`, `spec/evidence-claim-boundaries.md`, `spec/iot-profiles.md`.
- **Bottleneck:** Hiding later evidence does not remove device-class/evidence-type, verifier selection, packet-size and cadence fingerprinting before authentication. A valid cached signature does not guarantee freshness after reboot, rollback, stale reference values or untrusted time.
- **Distinct idea:** Stage-four-cohort *optional* observer experiment: (A) attestation-free/DNS-free core baseline, (B) early negotiation but no evidence, (C) fresh nonce-bound evidence, (D) cached Passport result plus resumed/restarted re-attestation. Pin passive/active/peer vantage and timing, record device-class and actual same-device labels independently, false positives, top-k reidentification, entropy loss, population priors, held-out firmware/devices, size/CPU/energy. Test signed stale cache after restart/rollback with no trustworthy clock, deny current authorization unless independent freshness proof exists.
- **Evidence maturity:** `concept`; no packet capture, classifier outcome, ZK-ARCHE side channel, measured MCU profile or executed freshness check.
- **Limitations:** LAKE EAD message-stage privacy does not automatically describe ZK-ARCHE's different wire design. Linkability advantage is a test hypothesis, not an empirical result. Cached-result policies depend on chosen deployment time source.
- **Inference:** Keep optional attestation disabled/absent from mandatory profile unless human-approved, measured and privacy-qualified. Attestation validity, claim age, revocation and local authorization must remain independent.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy:** No baseline change. Optional pre-auth advertisement/evidence increases packet length, parser/buffer pressure, hashing/signature CPU, storage and privacy surface; padding/re-attestation may increase latency/energy. No mandatory RTC, verifier or online service; numbers unmeasured.
- **Rust/C/vectors/profile:** Existing parser/0x0001/profile/vectors unchanged. Proposed optional conformance: wrong/verifier key, early-vs-late evidence stage, expiry/nonce, reboot/rollback, source-correlated metadata, and current policy negative fixtures, subject to human approval and measured target ceilings.
- **Next evidence / disposition:** Exact observer captures with matched no-attestation control, held-out metrics, no-clock signed-cache rollback tests and hardware resource data. `reproduce`; weekly candidate yes.

## 6. Cross-source synthesis

RFC 9334 formalizes separate evidence, verifier appraisal and relying-party decision; RFC 9711 provides a claims format; LAKE -07 demonstrates two binding timings. No outside framework is a trust root for ZK-ARCHE. The worthwhile R-006 question is whether a bounded, independently authorized optional attestation profile could add useful assurance without compromising no-learning P2P operation. The worthwhile R-015 question is whether earlier capability exchange creates an observable device fingerprint despite protected evidence. Both require negative controls and exact-head measurements.
