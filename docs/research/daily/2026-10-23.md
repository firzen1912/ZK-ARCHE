## 5. Findings

### D20261023-F01 — Early IoT startup fingerprinting changes the sampling-window contract

- **Novelty/owner:** new-to-queue/refines; R-015; R-005/R-009 cost and security cross-links; TD-002 and TD-004 claim boundaries.
- **Source/date/type:** Ciechonski et al., *Early-Stage IoT Device Identification Using Passive Network Traffic Analysis*; original peer-reviewed IFIP Networking 2026 paper (**24–27 May 2026** conference); author-accepted paper and metadata, https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10227830/ .
- **Verified source claim:** Passive flow-level metadata identified studied IoT devices with reported accuracy **up to 99% over 37 devices** in the first seconds of network attachment; extending observation did not consistently improve results.
- **Repository fact / bottleneck:** R-015 targets across-session linkability but has no boot-epoch trace corpus. `iot-core` is not selectable and has unresolved replay-epoch restart state; protocol privacy draft refuses treating restart as automatic unlinkability.
- **Distinct engineering idea / ZK-ARCHE inference:** Require **paired short-window and steady-state** passive traces over cold boot, trusted replay-state restore, `CONTINUITY_BROKEN` no-output, sleep/wake, retry, full AUTH, resumed association, and key/authorization refresh. Compare first 1/3/5/10 seconds (or equivalent first-N packets) to a steady-state and long-horizon baseline with matched device/transport cohorts. Label separately **device class** and **same-peer linkage**; neither proves the other.
- **Evidence maturity:** externally-reviewed for the source study; **concept/reproduce** for ZK-ARCHE.
- **Limitations:** Reported 99% is not applicable to this repository or to person reidentification; peer populations, background traffic, firmware, adapters, capture points and class priors materially change risk.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy impact:** initial measurement adds no wire, cryptographic dependency, device RAM/flash or trust; off-device capture/classifier costs storage/CPU. Future padding/buckets/jitter can add bytes, latency, energy and MCU state and may worsen retries/DoS. Never delay entropy failure, delete replay state, or emit dummy authenticated success to hide a branch.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile compatibility:** preserve existing proof and state vectors and unselected iot-core profile. Future sanitized metadata-only differential cases record public profile, transport, boot epoch, packet direction/size, timing, retry, completion/silence and baseline labels; retain no live identity, secret or private credential.
- **Next evidence:** local exact-head authorized Rust/C and physical board startup/steady captures, distinct sessions and devices in held-out test sets, measured top-k accuracy, false positives, entropy loss, cohort/priors, confidence intervals and any mitigation costs.
- **Disposition:** reproduce; weekly finding candidate **yes**.

### D20261023-F02 — Network observer vantage is an explicit reproducibility dimension

- **Novelty/owner:** new-to-queue/refines; R-015.
- **Source/date/type:** Rob Jansen, *CellShift: RTT-Aware Trace Transduction for Real-World Website Fingerprinting*; peer-reviewed **NDSS 2026**, official author/research page updated **2026-10-02**, https://www.robgjansen.com/publications/cellshift-ndss2026.html .
- **Verified source claim:** The work extracts timing/RTT metadata to transform Tor exit-side traces into a closer approximation of an entry-side observer’s trace, improving fingerprinting evaluation in its Tor datasets.
- **Repository fact / bottleneck:** ZK-ARCHE has no retained dual-vantage passive capture / RTT-normalization evidence. A ProVerif success or Rust/C packet parser test does not imply indistinguishability to a Wi-Fi/UDP/gateway observer.
- **Distinct engineering idea / ZK-ARCHE inference:** Every privacy trace corpus needs a **vantage manifest**: device vs gateway vs transport-peer/radio observer, visible IP/MAC/session/TLS fields, clock sync accuracy, RTT, loss/retransmission and measurement window. Compare same classifier on paired captures from ≥2 observer locations. Report matched-vantage and mismatched-vantage accuracy separately; explore RTT shifts only when supported by paired IoT captures.
- **Evidence maturity:** externally-reviewed in a Tor-specific setting; **concept** for ZK-ARCHE.
- **Limitations:** CellShift does not demonstrate an IoT or ZK-ARCHE exploit. Observer positions can change *visible fields*, not merely timing. Datasets must avoid inadvertent leakage from filenames, capture clocks or ground-truth labels.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy impact:** research-only offline analysis, no device wire/CPU/RAM/flash, crypto dependencies or trust-model change. Later traffic shaping may cost radio bytes, latency/energy, and would need separate benchmark gates.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile compatibility:** current canonical vectors unaffected. Proposed future test fixture adds observer timestamp variance, feature visibility, held-out physical device IDs, loss/retry treatment and anonymized metadata-provenance hashes.
- **Next evidence:** paired time-synchronized exact-head captures at two owned test vantage points; controlled RTT/loss experiments, realistic priors, session/device-holdout cross validation, top-k/false positive distribution and confidence intervals.
- **Disposition:** reproduce; weekly finding candidate **yes**.
