## 2. Executive synthesis

1. **Order-level PoP is narrower than authority.** The 2026-11-01 ACME WG PoP -01 draft requires distinct identifier authorization and possession authorization, binds challenge proof to the exact original order bytes, and freezes the accepted key before issuance. For ZK-ARCHE, use it as an adversarial ENROLL witness-key / commissioner-scope / storage-commit comparator, not an ACME or PKI dependency.
2. **Clockless voucher freshness cannot be inferred from expiry fields.** The ANIMA WG cBRSKI -32 draft distinguishes nonce-bound vouchers from nonceless vouchers whose expiry requires trustworthy time. Its re-enrollment trust-anchor replacement rejects partial/invalid chain changes. ZK-ARCHE should test nonce/operation-ID one-time freshness and atomic local authority handoff separately from a clock-dependent mode.
3. **Short AEAD tags demand a separate failed-authentication budget.** cBRSKI recommends closing a DTLS connection at 2^7 (=128) failed-authentication records when using TLS_AES_128_CCM_8_SHA256 with the specified safeguard. This is **not** a universal ZK-ARCHE counter limit: the valid engineering lesson is to gate each actual suite/profile by its own authenticating-decryption `v` and encryption `q` accounting.
4. **No novelty inflation:** both drafts were published November 1–2, 2026; cBRSKI -32 changelog calls its -32-specific updates mostly discovery/examples/editorial, and ACME PoP -01 describes editorial/normative refinements before WGLC. These are newly applied comparison oracles for this backlog, not newly invented security primitives or a claimed ZK-ARCHE attack.
5. No local Rust/C executable conformance run, physical MCU measurement, new formal proof or production claim was established. R-009 and R-008 remain `reproduce`; no new mandatory online manufacturer service, CA, ACME, EST, CoAP, DTLS or additional crypto suite was adopted.

### D20261113-F01 — Order-bound private-key possession is not an enrollment authorization

- **Novelty / selected item:** refines R-008; cross-reference R-013.
- **Source / date / type:** IETF ACME WG, *ACME Extension for Proof-of-Possession*, `draft-ietf-acme-pop-01`, **2026-11-01**, Standards Track Internet-Draft (work in progress): https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-acme-pop-01 , especially §§3–7, 9.
- **Verified source-supported claim:** The draft separately requires certificate-identifier authorization and order-bound `pop` private-key authorization. A new order fixes `popKey`, carries one non-reusable `pop-01` challenge, and moves to `ready` only after both kinds are valid. Its signature PoP uses a 32-byte server nonce and `SHA-256(raw_newOrder)`, hashing the exact decoded JWS payload octets rather than re-serialized JSON; KEM PoP uses a fresh challenge encapsulation, derived MAC key and HMAC over the same order digest. The issued key must match the accepted key. The PoP proves possession, not control of an arbitrary identifier or policy right.
- **Existing owner / anchor:** R-008/R-013, TD-004; `rust/crates/proto/src/enrollment_grant.rs`, `c/src/proto/enrollment_grant.c`, `spec/lineage-replace-verified-commit-contract.md`.
- **Bottleneck:** Post-PoP late key/subject substitution or treating successful possession as commissioner authorization during a multi-stage enrollment.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Add a *typed immutable enrollment attempt* oracle `(operation_id, subject_key, issuer/domain, target_audience, requested_rights, commissioner_generation, challenge_nonce, possession_context)`; all verified fields must be locked before an `AUTHORIZED` transition; a committed successor must be the same key/authority tuple. A new attempt may start only under explicit replay/persistence policy, never as a repair of failed completion.
- **Evidence maturity:** concept (published WG draft); existing ZK-ARCHE Rust/C decision-classifier vectors are software evidence for narrower predicates, not this full operation-to-storage trace.
- **Limitations:** Work-in-progress ACME server/CA scheme and its JWS/DER SPKI formats are not ZK-ARCHE wire requirements. The -01 document revision does not establish an independently reviewed ZK-ARCHE cryptographic proof.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Subject PoP and account/commissioner right are independent; normal AUTH cannot create trust; on a stale/changed key or context, discard provisional state and deny durable mutation.
- **Likely impact:** wire **none baseline**; optional new operation proof would add nonce/challenge bytes and flight count; extra per-attempt immutable state consumes RAM and durable replay metadata/flash; KEM mode would add decapsulation CPU/RAM and dependency, **not adopted**; trust isolation improves, privacy must account for stable keys.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile impact:** No current vector change. Proposed matched positive valid-issuance and negative wrong-subject-key, post-ready substitution, replayed proof from another operation, wrong commissioner/audience, interrupted commit and stale-generation tests; exact `iot-core` bytes require prior human specification.
- **Required next evidence:** Locally execute Rust/C staged operation-to-verified-commit tests with an independent verifier of accepted key/authority and failure/rollback cuts; retain exact source/revision/build and vector identities.
- **Disposition:** reproduce; **weekly candidate:** yes.

### D20261113-F02 — Clockless constrained vouchers and trust-anchor rollover require distinct recovery proofs

- **Novelty / selected item:** refines R-008; cross-reference R-005/R-013.
- **Source / date / type:** IETF ANIMA WG, *Constrained Bootstrapping Remote Secure Key Infrastructure (cBRSKI)*, `draft-ietf-anima-constrained-voucher-32`, **2026-11-02**, Standards Track Internet-Draft (work in progress): https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-anima-constrained-voucher-32 , §§6.7, 8–9; -32 change log predominantly editorial/discovery updates.
- **Verified source-supported claim:** cBRSKI's pledge voucher-request example omits `created-on` when the constrained device lacks real-time timekeeping. A nonce-bearing voucher binds to the immediate joining flow; a nonceless voucher example instead relies on `expires-on` and requires trusted current time to validate that expiry. The draft supports pinning a registrar raw public key instead of a complete certificate for constrained use. On unsuccessful re-enrollment into changed trust anchors, it requires the client not to install the new credential or anchors and to abort.
- **Existing owner / anchor:** R-008/R-005; `spec/lifecycle-persistence-freshness.md`, `spec/authorization-generation-lifecycle.md`, `spec/lineage-replace-verified-commit-contract.md`.
- **Bottleneck:** Lab devices without trusted clocks can accidentally treat expiry strings, cached signed vouchers or resettable operation IDs as proof of fresh authority; torn trust-anchor replacement can create a cross-domain permission.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Qualify two *mutually explicit* enrollment freshness contracts: local nonce/operation-ID-bound one-time commissioning with durable consume/rollback, versus trusted-clock-qualified expiry only when the platform demonstrably provides trusted time. Across both, use a predecessor/current-authority -> verified-successor -> staged durable commit -> activation -> predecessor retirement oracle; an untrusted signed voucher must never bypass local issuer/domain/scope authorization.
- **Evidence maturity:** concept/draft comparator; no ZK-ARCHE target evidence.
- **Limitations:** cBRSKI depends on manufacturer identity/MASA and a registrar/PKI domain; these are incompatible as mandatory components of ZK-ARCHE's infrastructure-free common P2P floor. The documented nonce and example do not prove full constrained-device power-loss durability.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Domain identity and approved local commissioner are distinct from possession of manufacturer-signed artifact; without trusted time, only an independently enforced one-time continuity rule can substantiate freshness.
- **Likely impact:** wire **none baseline**; optional voucher can increase payload and packet count, RPK may reduce certificate size but exact savings are unmeasured; extra flash for nonce consumption/anchors and CPU for signature/chain validation; time hardware/MASA/PKI dependency is **not adopted**; explicit domain separation preserves local data sovereignty and prevents unreviewed trust promotion.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile impact:** Existing 33 enrollment-grant decision cases do not establish restart/flash/time guarantees. Propose Rust/C paired no-clock/nonce-present vs nonceless/expiry-without-clock denies; expired-with-trusted-clock denies; wrong-registrar key, swapped authority, interrupted anchor rollover and same operation-ID-after-restart denies, plus positive explicitly authorized fresh issuance.
- **Required next evidence:** Exact-head simulated fault-cut and eventually physical ESP32-S3/STM32 power-loss/monotonicity/flash-wear measurements; human selection of any future concrete voucher encoding.
- **Disposition:** reproduce; **weekly candidate:** yes.

### D20261113-F03 — Short-tag DTLS authentication failures have an independently bounded budget

- **Novelty / selected item:** new-to-backlog quantitative comparator for R-009; not a new ZK-ARCHE cryptographic requirement.
- **Source / date / type:** IETF ANIMA WG cBRSKI -32, **2026-11-02**, §§6.1.2/DTLS 1.3 cipher suites, https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-anima-constrained-voucher-32 ; underlying Standards Track RFC 9147, *DTLS 1.3*, **2022-04**, Appendix B.3, https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9147.html .
- **Verified source-supported claim:** For `TLS_AES_128_CCM_8_SHA256`, cBRSKI notes RFC 9147's forgery/record-verification concern and recommends limiting failed authentication records to at most `2^7 = 128` before closing the DTLS connection, in its specific short-lived onboarding context. The draft does not claim this number governs arbitrary AEAD suites, implementations or ZK-ARCHE sessions.
- **Existing owner / anchor:** R-009; `spec/iot-profiles.md`, `spec/replay-continuity.md`, `spec/lifecycle-persistence-freshness.md`.
- **Bottleneck:** Accounting only sent encrypted records (`q`) while permitting unbounded adversary-driven failed decryptions/forgeries (`v`), or resetting a cryptographic budget after socket/address restart.
- **Distinct engineering idea:** Define a suite/profile-specific `(key_generation, direction, q, v)` lifetime ledger with *separate limits and actions*; count actual AEAD verification failures after early replay/parser rejection, do not conflate unauthenticated packet volume with completed AEAD verifies, and trigger a safe terminal/renegotiation state without accepting stale replay/authz/epoch.
- **Evidence maturity:** Standards Track DTLS RFC plus WG draft applicability guidance, but ZK-ARCHE test status remains concept-only.
- **Limitations:** The 128-record recommendation depends on CCM_8's 64-bit tag and DTLS context; it is neither a general AEAD security theorem nor directly transferable to ZK-ARCHE's current selected cryptography. Denial-of-service risk from attacker-triggered limit exhaustion must be measured.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference:** Separate conservative per-key forgery attempts and message encryptions, compose with replay-cache persistence and generation-aware rekey; qualified proof must not accidentally permit a cheap adversary-triggered unlimited rekey or downgrade.
- **Likely impact:** wire **none baseline**; local integer counters and safe hard-stop state cost RAM and possibly flash-write/endurance, and failure telemetry costs CPU; no mandated new crypto dependency; failure response classes can leak operational state and require privacy-equivalence measurements.
- **Rust/C/vector/profile impact:** No current suite/limits changed. Proposed paired `v=limit-1,limit,limit+1` accept/close/deny controls for each applicable suite; parser reject vs AEAD failure accounting, replay reject, abort/rekey/restart/rollback and cross-direction separation, with positive legal traffic retained.
- **Required next evidence:** Formal per-suite bound derived from concrete AEAD limits and deployment sender count/traffic, identical Rust/C instrumented counters and exact-head negative fixtures; constrained MCU memory/CPU/flash/energy and adversarial DoS measurements.
- **Disposition:** reproduce; **weekly candidate:** yes.

## 6. Cross-source synthesis

The two current drafts treat possession, identity rights, trust-anchor acceptance, and live cryptographic channel protection as **distinct gates**. For ZK-ARCHE, an ENROLL may proceed only with local commissioner authority plus exact subject-key/operation binding, while a fresh nonce/valid credential or live protected channel alone does not supply those rights. A constrained link's optional short-tag cipher must simultaneously enforce its own AEAD forgery bound without weakening replay, resumption, epoch or authorization continuity. The sources are **comparators**, not ZK-ARCHE interoperability results or requirements; cBRSKI's MASA/CA dependency and ACME's online CA account/order model cannot become mandatory in the offline P2P baseline.
