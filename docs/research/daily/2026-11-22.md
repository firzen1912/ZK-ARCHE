The author **explicitly corrected** the initial allegation of wrong proof verdict: the lemma is in fact true given the empty antecedent; missing diagnostics are the defect. Original #916 was closed because a more context-aware fix is proposed in #926. #926 describes preserving valid SAPIC-to-ProVerif export diagnostics while checking actions present in a process; it remains **open**, not merged on the inspected branch.

Compare native ProVerif event reachability and any future Tamarin/SAPIC export; do not accept a warning-only pass or an unattested \`verified\` label. Confirm exact exported actions before interpreting syntax-preserving translation as semantics-preserving.

- **Limitations:** Tamarin-only issue; cannot infer that ProVerif 2.05 has this bug. A nonexistent-event secrecy lemma is semantically true, not a counterexample to soundness. A reachable event alone does not prove the property for all relevant attack traces, and event manifests do not substitute cryptographic soundness.
- **ZK-ARCHE inference / resource/trust/privacy impact:** No wire/CPU/RAM/flash cost on devices, no provider or trust-root dependency; local formal qualification adds model preflight, negative-mutant execution, proof artifacts and review time. Avoid increasing privacy/zero-knowledge claims until the separate R-015/TD-001 evidence is obtained.
