The draft's resumption-chain PCS requires new uncompromised ephemerals and erasure; active compromise of the PSK may permit impersonation. No ZK-ARCHE PCS, resumption, or KCI security proof follows.
5. **No implementation promotion:** R-009/R-013/R-015 stay P0/`reproduce`. The repository's resumption lifecycle is logical, wire-unassigned and non-selectable; no LAKE EAD, PSK or EDHOC dependency added.

### D20261116-F02 — Compact resumption locator is not unique, bounded, or authoritative by default

- **Source/date/type:** IETF LAKE WG [LAKE-PSK -11](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-lake-edhoc-psk-11) ``3.1.1, 5.3.3, 6 and Appendix B; 2026-11-15 active Internet-Draft. The candidate/identifier behavior predates -11.
- **Verified claim:** `ID_CRED_PSK` can locate multiple `(PSK,CRED_I,CRED_R)` tuples, and responder can try another after failed first AEAD verification; exporter-derived `rKID` `kid_length` defaults to **2 bytes**. It is not globally unique peer identity or authorization.
- **Independent conditional derivation:** For `n` independently uniform, concurrently live handles from one 2^16-value authority namespace, birthday probability `1 - product_(i=0..n-1)(1 - i/65536)` is about **7.28% at n=100; 49.61% at n=300; 99.95% at n=1000**. These are assumptions-based probabilities, not real ZK-ARCHE fleet collision counts. Namespace partition, active handle count and dedup change the risk.
- **Owner/anchor/bottleneck:** R-013, cross-ref R-014, `spec/resumption-lifecycle.md`, `spec/security-considerations.md`. Registry collision can enlarge expensive trial sets or confuse unrelated authority domains; a handle never creates credential trust.
- **Engineering idea:** Benchmark optional **2/4/8-byte** handles with per-authority namespace scopes and active candidate counts, collisions, mismatched keys/hash algorithms, cross-audience identities, deliberately duplicated labels, CPU/RAM and timing-oracle bounds. Fail closed on excessive candidate count, don't silently choose a wrong record or mutate trust.
- **Maturity/limits/inference:** `concept` plus exact combinatorial math. No constrained deployment benchmark, constant-time claim or adopted ZK-ARCHE handle. Any future compact-reference design needs profile-specific cardinality and candidate-work budget.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy:** **Current unchanged**. Optional raw-handle 2→4/8 bytes adds 2/6 raw bytes before framing; multi-key attempts cost CPU, candidate storage costs RAM/flash, distinguishable failures may leak identity; unmeasured. No mandatory dependency.
- **Rust/C/vectors/profile:** Current 0x0001 unaffected; require matched valid, collision, wrong-key, cross-domain, over-cap, reject/accept vectors and STM32/ESP32-S3 evidence before any extension.
- **Disposition/next evidence/weekly:** `reproduce`; quantified namespace/candidate benchmark and decision corpus; yes.

### D20261116-F03 — Ratchet freshness does not imply safe durable retirement or active compromise recovery

- **Source/date/type:** IETF LAKE WG [LAKE-PSK -11](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-ietf-lake-edhoc-psk-11) ``5.4, 6, 9.3, 9.10–9.11; 2026-11-15 Internet-Draft; [RFC 9528](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9528.html), 2024 standardized EDHOC exporter comparator. Message/key mechanics pre-existing before -11.
- **Verified claim:** Initiator shall not **persistently store** `PRK_out`/application keys before authenticated fourth-message or equivalent protected response. For a resumption session using `rPSK_i`, responder may remove `rPSK_(i-1)` after verifying message3, initiator may remove `rPSK_i` after verifying fourth message, responder may remove `rPSK_i` after optional authenticated fifth message. Retain original identities/hash for resumption. Conditional PCS of successor ratchet needs fresh uncompromised ephemerals and prior key erasure. Shared PSK compromise also permits active impersonation (KCI); not a generalized post-compromise proof.
- **Owner/anchor/bottleneck:** R-009 P0, `spec/resumption-lifecycle.md`, `spec/replay-continuity.md`, `spec/authorization-generation-lifecycle.md`, TD-003. These already demand fail-closed current authority, replay/key epochs and durable bounded-use state but provide **no** resumption wire.
- **Engineering idea:** Construct a **two-peer/two-generation fault matrix** at message3/4/optional 5 confirmation, before/after each party's durable successor commit and predecessor erase. Check asymmetry after loss/retry/crash/restart, no unauthorized effect before confirmation, replay budget, old-key rejection, current revocation/policy and only independently authorized full-AUTH fallback. Separate state possession, cryptographic confirmation, durable commitment and safe old-key retirement.
- **Maturity/limits/inference:** `concept` external draft; no local ratchet, formal property or MCU flash-wear measurement. ZK-ARCHE must not inherit LAKE message formats, rPSK identifiers, PCS, quantum or KCI guarantees.
- **Wire/CPU/RAM/flash/dependency/trust/privacy:** Current unchanged; optional confirmation exchange adds message bytes/latency, ephemeral operations cost CPU, two-generation retention costs temporary RAM/flash and wear, identifiers may link sessions; require physical numbers. No baseline infrastructure dependency.
- **Rust/C/vectors/profile:** 0x0001 unchanged. Any future selection requires pinned equivalent Rust/C state and negative packet/persistence vectors plus human-approved wire/protocol design.
- **Disposition/next evidence/weekly:** `reproduce`; exact-head dual-party crash/late-packet/authorization corpus and limits on PCS/KCI; yes.
